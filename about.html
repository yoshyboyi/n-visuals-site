About

The person, the camera, and the reason for standing pitchside every weekend.

Hi, I'm Jeremy

Im a photographer with almost a year of experience and i enjoy taking photos and have worked for clubs like Kalamunda city and armadale soccer club. Im hoping to continue my journey to the A league

What I shoot

I provide professional football photography for players, teams, and leagues, tailored to the needs of whoever I’m working with. From match-day action and team photography to individual player portfolios, I aim to capture high-quality images that showcase the intensity, personality, and moments that define the game.

Equipment & approach

I shoot with a Sony a6000 paired with a Tamron 28–200mm lens, giving me the versatility to capture everything from close-up player moments to action across the pitch. I typically arrive around 30 minutes before kick-off to prepare my equipment, meet the teams and staff, and capture some pre-match moments before the action begins.
